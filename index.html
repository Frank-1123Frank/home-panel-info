Private local dashboard

Home Panel

Home Panel is a lightweight touchscreen dashboard designed to run locally on a household Windows device.

Google Calendar access

With the user’s permission, Home Panel reads upcoming events from calendars selected by the user and displays them on the local dashboard. Calendar access is read-only: Home Panel cannot create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events.

Designed for privacy

  • Home Panel runs locally on the user’s own device.
  • Google Calendar information and authorization tokens are stored locally.
  • Google Calendar data is not sent to the developer or sold to third parties.
  • There is no advertising, analytics, or telemetry.
  • The Google account can be disconnected from within Home Panel.
